      How to play

      Guess the city from its 3D skyline in 6 tries.

      Move: W A S D or arrow keys (hold Shift to go faster). Look: drag. Zoom: scroll or pinch. Click the map first so the keys don't type in the guess box.

      Every wrong guess shows you more: the camera pulls back, circles the city, then rises above it. Later clues reveal the continent and country.
